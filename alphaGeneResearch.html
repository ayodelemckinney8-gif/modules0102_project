FAQs about the Alpha Gene

Is the "Omega Gene Theory" a legitimate scientific concept?

No, the "Omega Gene Theory" is not a legitimate scientific concept. The disgraced scholar Xavier House propogated this conspiracy theory in an attempt to discredit C.H.A.G.'s AGR. Since its introduction into the public domain, countless studies and respected Alpha Geniuses have discovered little evidence to support it. The Alpha Gene is increasingly believed to be the final step in human evolution. It is virtually impossible for the human body to safely sustain the amount of energy it would hypothetically require to generate any known or unknown Alpha ability.


What is a Stateless Alpha Gifted Agent (SAGA) Tag?

A SAGA Tag is an official waiver of a repeatedly violent Alpha Gifted Agent's (AGA) human rights and privileges, as outlined in the U.N. SAGA Charter passed in 1954. The emergence of the Alpha Gene led to a worldwide increase in violence, crime, and corruption. The Euro AG Apocalypse of 1936 showed the world that we had much to learn as a species regarding the Alpha Gene. Over the next 18 years, it became evident that humanity needed to work on one accord to ensure our safety. The SAGA Charter was passed to reinforce our commitment to peace and harmony. Such an extreme measure has proven successful; the world has been experiencing an unprecendented period of peace for the last 50 years. A SAGA Tag signifies to law enforcement to rely on C.H.A.G. agents to apprehend a subject. It also grants states the right to use any force deemed necessary to apprehend a subject. A SAGA Tag is not a death sentence, but it does signify that the subject is considered a threat to humanity and should be treated as such.
