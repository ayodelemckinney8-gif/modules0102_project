Bio

Codename: Switch
Name: Faith Amira
Age: 34
Alpha Class: PHYSIO Class 5, Superior Speed QUANTA Class 2, "Pockets"
K-Category: B

Subject Information

A rogue C.H.A.G. operative responsible for colluding with Amari Cloud, codename Fortune Cookie and codename Black Moses for 2013's Horsemen Leak. She finished second in her C.H.A.G. Academy cohort, also becoming the second youngest C.H.A.G. agent in history. Switch is reported to be a tactical genius and lethal CQ combatant. She utilizes tools such as cargo pants, assortments of bags, jackets, and other item carriers to maximize the efficiency of her "Pockets." Her previous C.H.A.G. files suggest anti-social tendencies originating from Operation Crowd Silencer. These earlier files, plus reports from both of her field partners and codename Aero, lend us to believe she is a lone operator. Despite this, she's killed or injured 60 agents over a ten year span.
