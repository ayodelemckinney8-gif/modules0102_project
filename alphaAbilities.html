Alpha Abilities

In 1901, the first human with an Alpha Gene was born. After that, every human was born with an Alpha Gene. This gene grants humans 1 to 2 random abilities. By 1950, humans with an Alpha Gene, by then called Alpha-Gifted, outnumbered normal humans 5:2. The advent of the Alpha-Gifted led to the technological advancement of the human race. The internet was invented in 1936. The first manned space exhibitions began in 1939. By 1955, world hunger was solved, and the infant mortality rate was 0.01%.


The AG Classification and Categorization System was invented in 1939 by the first recorded Alpha Genius, Dr. Xavier House.

Things You Should Know about the Politics of the Alpha Gene

The Central Hub for Alpha Gifted may have been officially formed in 1951, but most historians agree the Euro Alpha War is the agency's precursive event. Euro Alpha War (1936) was a brief skirmish between the USSR, Germany, the UK, Poland, Czechoslovakia, Romania, and France. They fought over an eight month period after German and USSR joint offensives against Poland and Czechoslovakia. The Germany-USSR collaborative had the objective of conquering Eastern Europe (Austria, Hungary, and Poland would be assimilated into Germany while Czechoslovakia and Romania would be controlled by the USSR). In month three, the German-Russian pact dissolved over a dispute on how to handle non-AGA combatants. The Germans sought prison camps while the Russians wanted forced assimilation, resulting in the first all out "Alpha War," in which each participating country fought primarily on its own behalf and with the reckless use of Alpha abilities. The use of Alpha Abilities had never occurred at this level. Most of Eastern Europe suffered over $28 trillion in damages. A ceasefire was called after a Russian soldier with suspected QUANTA Class 1 abilities was killed in Romania. Upon his death, the soldier released radioactive energy over 24,000 sq mi, or an area roughly the size of West Virginia. Leaders from all participating countries realized they had used pre-Alpha Gene war strategies and tactics with abilities the world still didn’t understand. Peace was declared and the European Union was founded in 1938 to foster safety throughout the continent, rebuild Eastern Europe’s infrastructure and economy, and strengthen cultural bonds.

Political discourse is an internationally acknowledged civil right. The marketplace of ideas is where C.H.A.G. found its identity. Political dissent, on the other hand, is a dangerous trap. We all hold power, and it's our responsibilities to our fellow man to control that power. When C.H.A.G., the U.N., and other science-backed and peace-keeping organizations create and implement policies, it's your human right to abide by them. Safety, caution, and control keep us safe. We must all fundamentally agree and abide by this reality. There are some groups that have emerged as dangerous anti-human advancement radicals. One of the most prominent of these groups is the Suppressors.
The Suppressors are malicious hackers and technologically-enhanced vigilantes acting as SAGA bounty hunters. These are highly trained and loosely connected international cells that hack local Registries to obtain your data. Suppressors use this knowledge to capture and experiment on or extrajudiciously kill SAGAs. If you suspect someone of being a Suppressor, immediately report them to your local authorities. Suppressors should never be allowed to walk the streets. A team of C.H.A.G. agents is always on standby to apprehend these political dissidents.
