Bio

Codename: Black Ice
Name: Davu Wanjala
Age: 30
Alpha Class: QUANTA Class 6, Photon Manipulation
MANIPULATION Class 1, Ice Zones
K-Category: D

Subject Information

Codename Black Ice is the youngest C.H.A.G. operative in history. Having graduated top of his class in 2008, he's enjoyed significant success as a field agent. His Ice Zones are a devastating ability. He has learned to control the weather of a half-mile diameter area, creating blizzards, ice storms, and cryokinetic constructs. His mastery is impressive, able to construct objects with an alarming attention to detail. He can control the wind patterns, guiding ice to form in unnatural ways. But, his mastery has come at a shameful cost. As a child, his lack of control killed or maimed numerous family members. He was unregistered, so his family never received the resources needed to keep them safe. Codename Aero found him when he was 6 years old. He continued to face hardships. He failed his initial acceptance exam for C.H.A.G. agent eligibility. His documented introduction to codename Switch corresponds with his rise to valedictorian. Is there a possibility he's compromised?
