C.H.A.G. Registry


Welcome to the Registry, the C.H.A.G. global database for information on all legal AGA abilities. Are you and your family registered? Check Your Registration Status Here.

The Central Hub for Alpha Gifted (C.H.A.G.) was created in 1951 to monitor humanity's evolution. The Registry was the organization's first initiative to track the Alpha Gene and ensure the world's safety. You can think of the Registry as a global phonebook for every man, woman, and child on Earth. In a world where we're all united through the shared miracle of the Alpha Gene, the Registry keeps us responsible with our gifts. Registering your abilities helps protect humanity in 5 key ways:

  1. It alerts C.H.A.G. to your needs and helps get you the appropriate resources for the safe management of your Alpha abilities.
  2. Registration updates C.H.A.G. agents in real time, meaning you help them move tactically in the field.
  3. It provides a comprehensive record of your Alpha abilities, which can be crucial for medical purposes.
  4. You actively contribute to cutting edge Alpha Gene research and development.
  5. You aid the effort to understand and contain dangerous emerging Alpha abilities.
Failure to register your Alpha abilities is a crime punishable by international law. Failure to fully disclose all of your abilities is also a crime punishable by international law. C.H.A.G. agents are trained to detect unregistered Alpha abilities, and they will pursue you if you are found to be in violation of the law. The parents/legal guardians of unregistered minors are punished to the full extent of the law. Unregistered individuals, regardless of intersectionality, are automatically tagged as Stateless Alpha Gifted Agents (SAGA). To learn more about the severity of a SAGA Tag, go to the FAQs on the Alpha Gene Research page. If you have any questions about the Registry, please contact your local C.H.A.G. office or meet your recruiter for more information.
