Bio

Codename: Aero
Name: Raheem Kapur
Age: 48
Alpha Class: PHYSIO Class 4, Binary Speed
K-Category: B

Subject Information

A prime example of mission loyalty and organizational commitment, codename Aero has served C.H.A.G. for 28 years. As a field agent from 1995-2009 and a recruiter from 2009-present, he has demonstrated a surprising tenacity given his relative weakness. Most reports suggest he's a revered CQ combatant. His strategic mind was cited as the leading reason for his pending promotable status for the C.H.A.G. Sentinel Program. Steadfast, honorable, and disciplined, codename Aero is an excellent role model for all agents. He's responsible for recruiting such talents as Black Ice, Pscythe, Overthought, Punchline, and many more.
